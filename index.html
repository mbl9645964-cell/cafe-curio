Our Story

A table for every mood.

Café Curio is built around one simple belief — good food brings people together. Warm terracotta tiles, woven rattan canopies, brass-framed emerald seating and a cobalt-blue bar turn every corner into a small discovery.

Start with a cappuccino, share a platter of tandoori paneer tikka, settle into a thali-style lunch or linger over a Rasmalai — there's always a seat that feels like yours.
